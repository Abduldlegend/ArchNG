Design Philosophy & Practice

Founded in 2021 following over a decade of tier-one practice between London and Lagos, Adaeze Okafor Studio champions an unyielding commitment to climate-responsive tropical modernism. Our practice treats the unique Atlantic microclimates of coastal Nigeria not as constraints to seal away behind hermetic HVAC systems, but as guiding physical conditions that dictate structural massing, porous masonry, and natural thermal air currents.

By stabilizing regional Nigerian laterite soils for load-bearing rammed earth walls and marrying them with locally milled sustainable teak, our residential residences achieve passive acoustic stillness and natural thermal moderation. We believe luxury in West African architecture lives in spatial proportions, deep perimeter shade verandas, and the authentic tactile resonance of indigenous material craftsmanship.

“True architectural longevity in the tropics begins when a building learns to breathe with the coastal wind rather than fighting it.”

— Adaeze Okafor, ARCON / NIA Principal
