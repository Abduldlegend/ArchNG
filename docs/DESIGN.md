## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a quiet, authoritative presence rooted in architectural rigor and warmth. Designed for Nigerian property clients, corporate commissioning bodies, and registered architectural practitioners, the platform balances prestige with accessibility. The visual identity draws from architectural monograph publishing, gallery curation, and structural drafting conventions. 

Key attributes:
- **Quiet Confidence:** Eliminates gratuitous decoration, dramatic gradients, and noisy drop shadows in favor of balanced typography, measured proportions, and intentional white space.
- **Warm Editorial Quality:** Inspired by heavyweight architectural print catalogues. Uses unbleached paper foundations rather than harsh synthetic whites.
- **Architectural Precision:** Subtle drafting accents—such as delicate hairline crosshairs (`+`) at framing corners and muted division lines—echo technical blueprint details without distracting from rich imagery.
- **Trust & Verification:** Highlights credentialed practitioners through clear, dignified verification signifiers, providing reassurance for substantial capital commitments.

## Colors

The palette is strictly tuned for a light-mode editorial canvas. It derives its balance from earthen minerals, sunbaked clay, and architectural materials.

- **Foundations:**
  - **Canvas Background (`#F7F4EE`):** Warm Paper foundation, providing soft ambient contrast against white surfaces and editorial photos.
  - **Surface Default (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used for elevated interactive cards, modal sheets, and form groups.
  - **Surface Alternate (`#EFEAE0`):** Muted sand for subtle section breaks, inactive tags, filter bars, and secondary modules.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:**
  - **Primary Ink (`#1C1B19`):** Dense charcoal-black for high legibility across editorial headings and primary copy.
  - **Secondary Stone (`#6A655C`):** Warm neutral grey for architectural metadata, captions, and secondary descriptions.
  - **Hairline Border (`#E3DDD0`):** Neutral separation line applied at 1px thickness across all component boundaries.
- **Accents & Semantics:**
  - **Primary Terracotta (`#B8502D`):** Earthy accent reserved for primary conversion actions, active filter highlights, and critical links.
  - **Verified & Success (`#1F7A5A`):** Deep architectural evergreen denoting verified NIA/ARCON licensure and completed demo bookings.
  - **Warning (`#B7791F`):** Ochre gold indicating pending verifications or profile updates.
  - **Error (`#B42318`):** Crimson for invalid input fields, submission errors, and scheduling conflicts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of a warm literary serif (`Newsreader`) with the crisp structural utility of `Inter`.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Newsreader` with optical sizing enabled. Titles use tight letter tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`) to mirror luxury spatial monographs.
- **Small-Caps Labels:** Category markers, metadata prefixes, and input labels use uppercase `Inter` with wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) for high scannability against neutral backgrounds.
- **Financial & Data Formats:** Naira currency symbols (₦) and budget metrics must explicitly utilize tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column alignment across project budget ranges and square-meter metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural modular grid structured on an 8pt base unit. 

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, 1440px maximum content constraint, `3rem` (48px) side margins, and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2rem` (32px) margins, and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Image Proportions:** Architectural photography strictly conforms to aspect ratios of **3:2** (landscape exterior/interior sweeps) or **4:5** (portrait structural details and architect headshots).
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Generous vertical section spacing (`space-xl` to twice `space-xl`) permits featured buildings and structural floor plans to breathe without viewport congestion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through surface juxtaposition and precise line work rather than cast shadows:

- **Hairline Framing:** All cards, dialogue panels, navigation headers, and segmented controls feature a solid `1px` border in `#E3DDD0`. Heavy drop shadows are omitted to preserve architectural flatness.
- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Base canvas sits on `#F7F4EE`.
  - Cards, dropdown lists, and modal drawers elevate visually by utilizing `#FFFFFF` fills against the `#F7F4EE` canvas.
  - Sub-panels, project feature tables, and inactive chips use `#EFEAE0`.
- **Architectural Drafting Accents:** Subtle `+` crosshair marks (8px x 8px hairline glyphs in `#6A655C`) may be positioned at the exterior corner intersections of primary project showcases or hero dividers.
- **Hover & Interaction Shift:** Hover states never push elements upward via shadow expansion. Instead, interactivity is communicated through border transitions from `#E3DDD0` to `#1C1B19` or background shifts from `#FFFFFF` to `#F7F4EE`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances human warmth with structural discipline:

- **Default Formats (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to interactive text inputs, project media containers, dropdowns, and card bodies.
- **Large Panels (1rem / 16px):** Reserved for modal overlays, presentation viewports, and primary showcase banners.
- **Pill Shapes (`rounded-full`):** Strictly reserved for verification status pills, categorical metadata chips, and circular media controls.
- **Dividers:** Clean, unembellished 1px continuous lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Video Demo / Inquire):** Background `#B8502D`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px corner radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Label style `label-md`. Hover state darkens background to `#9C3E1F`. Focus state introduces a 2px offset ring in `#B8502D`.
- **Secondary Action:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E3DDD0`, text `#1C1B19`. Hover state shifts background to `#EFEAE0`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, text `#1C1B19`, underline on hover.

### Badges & Verification Pills
- **Verified Architect Badge:** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), background `#E8F4F0`, 1px border `#1F7A5A` at 20% opacity. Text `#1F7A5A`, font size `12px`, semi-bold. Prefixed by a crisp 12px checkmark icon.
- **Category Chips:** Pill shape, background `#EFEAE0`, text `#6A655C`. When active: background `#1C1B19`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Architect & Project Cards
- **Architect Profile Card:** White background, 1px border `#E3DDD0`, 8px radius. Features a 4:5 portrait crop, verified pill anchored top-right, architect/firm name in `headline-sm` (`Newsreader`), followed by location and ARCON registration number in `body-sm` (`Stone #6A655C`).
- **Project Showcase Card:** White background, 3:2 landscape photograph with micro-border separation. Title in `title-md` with tabular budget indicators (`₦X,XXX,XXX`) aligned to the card base.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Input Fields:** White surface, 1px `#E3DDD0` border, 8px radius. Height 48px. Text `#1C1B19`. Placeholder text `#6A655C` at 60% opacity. Focus state shifts border color to `#1C1B19` with no outer glow.
- **Labels:** Positioned above inputs using `label-caps` (`11px`, bold, uppercase, tracked wide) in `#6A655C`.

### Video Demo Request Drawer
- **Drawer / Modal:** Fixed width slide-over or centered modal, white surface, hairline border `#E3DDD0`. Header features a `Newsreader` modal title, a structured project summary block in `#EFEAE0`, and clear time-slot grid selectors formatted with 1px border outlines.
