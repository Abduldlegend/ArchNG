Practice Ethos

Design Philosophy & Practice

“True architectural longevity in the tropics begins when a building learns to breathe with the coastal wind rather than fighting it.”

— Adaeze Okafor, ARCON / NIA Principal

Established in Ikoyi in 2011, Adaeze Okafor Studio focuses on high-performance residential estates that combine modern rammed earth masonry with natural ocean wind paths. By sourcing red laterite clay from southwestern Nigeria and using solar brise-soleil facades, the practice creates modern living spaces that stay 5°C cooler naturally without relying heavily on generator power.
