Architectural Narrative

Situated at the edge of the Epe lagoon wetlands, this residential monograph investigates the intersection between monolithic thermal mass and maritime vernacular building. Rather than importing energy-intensive concrete facades, the design specifies 450mm load-bearing walls composed entirely of stabilized laterite subsoil excavated directly from the building foundation.

The layout is structured around an internal thermal sink courtyard. Ocean breeze funnels off the lagoon surface, through shaded timber brise-soleil galleries, across an unheated lap pool, and induces natural stack ventilation upwards through high-level clerestory louvers.

Passive Microclimate Strategy
1
Lagoon Thermal Breeze Funnel

Aligns with seasonal southwesterly trade winds to achieve zero-energy daytime ventilation.

2
Thermal Mass Phase Shift

450mm compressed earth dampens peak diurnal solar gain by up to 7.4°C indoors.
